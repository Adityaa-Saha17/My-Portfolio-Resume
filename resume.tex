  \item Designed a \textbf{shared-mutex LRU cache} with TTL decay and negative caching, plus a per-IP \textbf{token-bucket rate limiter} to bound amplification abuse

  \item Implemented a \textbf{control unit} driving per-opcode signals, an \textbf{ALU control decoder}, and EX/MEM and MEM/WB \textbf{forwarding} with load-use interlock stalls
